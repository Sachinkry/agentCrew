# Freelancer Budget & Invoice Tracker

A complete financial management spreadsheet for freelancers.

## What's included
- Monthly income & expense tracker with 12 tabs (Jan–Dec)
- Invoice generator with auto-numbering (INV-001 format)
- Tax estimate calculator (quarterly)
- Client summary dashboard
- Runway calculator (months of savings at current burn)

## Row counts
- Income rows per month: 50
- Expense categories: 20
- Invoice template rows: 30

## How to use
1. Open in Excel or Google Sheets
2. Enter your hourly rate and monthly expenses in the "Settings" tab
3. Log income in each monthly tab
4. Use the Invoice tab to generate client invoices
